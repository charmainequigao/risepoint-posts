Paano magsimula bilang VA? Here's the trap almost every beginner falls into. Including me.

It looks like this:

Stage 1: Watch videos. Take free courses. Feel productive.
Stage 2: Build a profile. Rewrite it again and again kasi "hindi pa maganda."
Stage 3: Save more guides. Join more groups. Still haven't applied.
Stage 4: Feel like a failure, even though you never actually tried.

Sound familiar?

Learning feels safe. Applying doesn't. So we keep learning, and we call it "preparing."

But clients don't hire the person who studied the longest. They hire the person who showed up in their inbox with a clear offer.

Here's what I'd tell anyone who's been "preparing" for months:

You don't need one more course. You need one skill, three sample pieces, and the courage to send your first proposal. Then the next one. Then the next.

Hindi ka kulang sa kaalaman. Kulang ka sa proposals na naipadala.
