Do you need good English to be a virtual assistant in the Philippines? You don't need perfect English. You need clear written English, and written English can be edited before you send it.

A lot of VA work happens in writing: Slack messages, emails, Google Docs comments, short recorded videos with captions.

I know this is the biggest wall for a lot of you. "Pangit English ko, baka hindi para sa akin 'to."

Here's what I want you to understand: the fear usually isn't about English. It's the fear that they'll find out you're "not enough." English is just where that fear hides.

What actually helps:
• Write short, clear sentences. Simple beats fancy.
• Use Grammarly or a similar tool to check before sending.
• Keep a notes file of phrases clients use, and reuse them.
• Read your message out loud once before you hit send.

Clients from the US and Australia work with people from all over the world. They're used to different accents and styles. What they care about: did you understand the task, and did you deliver on time?

Your English will get better the more messages you send. Not before.
