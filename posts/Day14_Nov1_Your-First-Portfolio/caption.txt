VA portfolio for beginners in the Philippines: you don't need past clients to have a portfolio. You need proof that you can do the work.

A simple one-page portfolio is enough to start. Include:

1. A short intro: who you are and the service you offer.
2. Three sample pieces for that service. Practice work counts.
3. The tools you use.
4. How to contact you.

No paid clients yet? Make samples for a pretend business, or redo a real brand's post the way you'd improve it. Just label them as samples, and never present them as work you were hired for.

Where to put it: a free Canva website, a Google Drive folder with clear file names, or a simple PDF.

Keep it short. Clients skim. One clear page beats ten pages no one finishes.
