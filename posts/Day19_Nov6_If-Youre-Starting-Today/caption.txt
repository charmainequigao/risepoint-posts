If you're starting as a VA today, here's what I'd tell you. Walang sugarcoating.

1. You don't need to know everything. You need to know one thing well enough to help someone.

2. Your first client won't be your dream client. That's okay. They're your proof.

3. Rejection is part of the job, not a sign you should quit.

4. Your portfolio can be samples you made for practice. No one expects paid work on day one.

5. Charge something fair, even at the start. Free work forever doesn't build a business.

6. Show up even when no one's watching. Consistency is what clients notice.

7. Rest. Burnout won't make you faster.

8. Ask for help. You were never meant to figure this out alone.
