Free tools for virtual assistants: you don't need expensive software to start. These free tools cover most beginner VA work.

• Google Workspace (Gmail, Docs, Sheets, Calendar, Drive): the basics almost every client uses
• Canva: graphics, social posts, simple presentations
• Trello or ClickUp: managing tasks
• Grammarly: checking your writing before you send
• Loom: short screen-recorded videos for clients
• Meta Business Suite: scheduling Facebook and Instagram posts

Swipe for what each one is used for and where to learn it for free.

Don't try to learn all of them this week. Pick the ones that match the service you chose. Master two before adding more.
