VA proposal sample for beginners in the Philippines: your proposal doesn't need to be long. It needs 4 parts.

1. Show you read their post.
2. Name their problem.
3. Show how you'd solve it (with a small sample if you can).
4. Give one easy next step.

That's it. Short, specific, about them.

Swipe for the fill-in-the-blanks template and a sample you can adapt.

Don't copy it word for word. Clients can tell. Use it as a structure, and make every proposal about that specific client.

Save this for your next application. And comment PROPOSAL if you want feedback on yours.
