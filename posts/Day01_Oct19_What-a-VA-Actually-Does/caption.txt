What is a virtual assistant? Simply put: someone who helps a business remotely, from home, with tasks the owner doesn't have time for.

A lot of people hear "VA" and think it's a scam, or that it's just data entry. It's neither.

Here's what VA work really looks like:
• Answering emails and organizing calendars
• Scheduling social media posts
• Making simple graphics in Canva
• Replying to customer messages
• Updating spreadsheets and files

Who hires VAs? Small business owners, coaches, online shops, real estate agents and busy professionals, many of them in the US, Australia, the UK and Canada.

How do you get paid? Usually in dollars, through online payment platforms or bank transfer, then converted to pesos.
