How do virtual assistants in the Philippines get paid? This is one of the most common questions, so here's the simple version.

Most clients pay in US dollars. The money usually goes through one of these:
• PayPal
• Wise
• Payoneer
• Direct bank transfer
• The job platform itself (like Upwork), which you then withdraw from

From there, you transfer it to your Philippine bank or e-wallet, and it's converted to pesos.

A few honest tips:
• Compare fees and exchange rates before you choose. They add up.
• Set up your account early, before you land a client. Verification can take time.
• Never give anyone your login or pay a "fee" to receive your salary. That's a scam.
• Keep track of what you earn. Future you will thank you.
