"May trabaho pa ako, wala akong time."

I hear you. Here's how to start learning without quitting your job.

• Find your hour. Before work, lunch break, or after the kids sleep. One hour a day is enough to begin.
• Use your commute. Watch or listen to lessons on the jeep or bus.
• Weekends are for building. Use a few hours on Saturday to make your samples.
• Pitch at night. Many clients are in the US, so your evening is their morning.
• Don't quit yet. Build first, then decide.

It's slower this way. But slow and steady is still moving.

One hour a day adds up faster than you think.

What's your one free hour? Comment it below. Let's hold each other accountable.
