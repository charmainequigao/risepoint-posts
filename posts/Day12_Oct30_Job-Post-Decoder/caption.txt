How to read VA job posts: clients don't always say exactly what they mean. Here's a quick decoder so you know what you're applying for.

"Fast-paced environment" usually means lots of tasks and quick turnaround.
"Wear many hats" means a mix of admin, social media, maybe customer support.
"Detail-oriented" means mistakes really bother them. Double-check everything.
"Must overlap with EST" means part of your shift is at night, Philippine time.
"Long-term opportunity" can mean stable work. Ask if it starts with a paid trial.

And one that should make you stop: "Pay a training fee first." Real clients don't charge you to work for them.

Reading the post carefully is step one of a good proposal. When you show you understood what they need, you're already ahead of most applicants.

Save this for your next job search. What phrase have you seen that confused you? Comment below.
