But first, let's talk about the real issue.

"Wala akong experience" usually isn't about experience. It's about the fear of being rejected. Kasi kapag nag-apply ka at na-reject, parang na-confirm yung kinatatakutan mo about yourself.

Diplomas, years of experience, a polished portfolio: those are shortcuts clients use to decide faster. They're not the actual requirement.
