Online job for Filipina moms: 4 hours a day can be enough to start.

The math: $5/hour × 4 hours × 20 working days = $400. That's around ₱24,400 a month. Not a guarantee, but a realistic part-time target once you have a client.

Let's say you have three kids. The youngest naps after lunch and sleeps by 8 PM. The older two are in school from 8 to 3.

That's time. Maybe not a lot, pero nandiyan.

Here's what a lot of moms don't know: many clients in the US and Australia don't need you online at the same time as them. A lot of the work is asynchronous. They send the task, you do it, you send it back. They don't care if you finished it at 10 PM, basta tapos.

No bundy clock. No boss checking if you're at your desk.

And the best part: nasa bahay ka pa rin. If a kid gets sick, you're there. If there's a school program, you're there.

This isn't about doing everything at once. It's about choosing work that fits your life, instead of forcing your life to fit your work.
